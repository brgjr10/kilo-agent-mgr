The glass effect in index.html and desktop/frontend/*.html adapts the
displacement-map and feDisplacementMap technique from that project to a
dependency-free dashboard, with per-tier rendering and a size-bucketed filter
cache. The underlying idea — generating the displacement map as an inline SVG
and using it as a backdrop-filter so a surface's edge refracts what is behind it
— is Nik Delvin's.
